FAQ

Everything you need to know

How do I get started with a project?

Getting started is simple! Reach out to us through our contact form, email, or schedule a free consultation call. We’ll discuss your project goals, timeline, budget, and vision. After our initial conversation, we’ll send you a detailed proposal outlining the scope, deliverables, timeline, and investment required. Once you approve, we’ll kick off with a discovery session to dive deep into your brand.

What information do you need from me to start?

To create the most effective design solution, we’ll need:

  • Your business goals and target audience details
  • Current branding materials (if any)
  • Competitor information and market positioning
  • Design preferences and inspiration examples
  • Budget and timeline expectations
  • Any specific requirements or must-haves

The more information you share, the better we can tailor our approach to your unique needs.

Do you work with businesses of all sizes?

Absolutely! We’ve successfully partnered with everyone from ambitious startups and solo entrepreneurs to established corporations and Fortune 500 companies. Our experience across 500+ brands means we can scale our approach to fit your specific needs and budget, regardless of your company size.

How long does a typical branding project take?

Timelines vary based on project scope and complexity:

  • Logo Design: 1–2 weeks
  • Complete Brand Identity: 2–3 weeks
  • Packaging Design: 1–2 weeks
  • Full Brand Strategy + Identity: 2–3 weeks
  • Rebranding Projects: 2–3 weeks

Rush projects can be accommodated with adjusted timelines. We’ll provide a detailed schedule during our proposal phase with key milestones and delivery dates.

What does your design process look like?

Our proven process ensures exceptional results:

  • Step 1: Discovery — Deep dive into your business, competitors, and target audience through questionnaires and strategy sessions.
  • Step 2: Strategy & Research — Market research, mood boards, and strategic direction development.
  • Step 3: Concept Development — Initial design concepts based on our strategic foundation.
  • Step 4: Refinement — Collaborative revision process based on your feedback.
  • Step 5: Finalization — Final tweaks and preparation of all deliverable files.
  • Step 6: Delivery & Support — Complete file delivery and ongoing support to keep your brand consistent.
